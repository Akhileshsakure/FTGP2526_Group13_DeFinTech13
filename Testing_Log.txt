System Testing and Feature Validation Log

This document summarizes the comprehensive testing coverage and feature validation for our DeFi lending protocol. It covers the core functionalities, the advanced Aave-style risk management system, security features, and frontend integrations.

1. CEth (ETH Market) Core Flow Verification
File: CEthFlow.ts
This tests the fundamental lifecycle of the native ETH market:
- Successfully deployed all core contracts including the Comptroller, MockPriceOracle, and AaveInterestRateStrategy.
- Verified user ETH deposits (mint) to receive cETH tokens.
- Tested collateral activation by allowing users to enter the market.
- Validated the borrow and repayment lifecycle against the supplied collateral.

2. Redemption and Withdrawal Flow
File: MoreFlows.ts
Tested the exit logic of the protocol:
- Verified that users can redeem their cTokens to withdraw underlying assets.
- Confirmed that the underlying assets are correctly transferred back to the user wallet and the corresponding cTokens are burned, successfully decreasing the user's supplied balance.

3. ERC20 Market Integration
File: MoreFlows.ts
Verified the protocol's compatibility with standard ERC20 tokens:
- Deployed a MockERC20 token and handled the approval process.
- Successfully executed mint, borrow, and repay flows using the ERC20 token.
- Confirmed that cross-asset borrowing (e.g., supplying ETH to borrow ERC20) functions securely.

4. Liquidation Mechanics
File: MoreFlows.ts
Simulated a realistic DeFi risk scenario to test system solvency:
- A user supplied collateral and borrowed against it.
- Artificially lowered the asset price via the MockPriceOracle to simulate a market crash.
- Confirmed that the account correctly entered a shortfall state.
- Verified that a third-party liquidator could execute the liquidateBorrow function, successfully seizing the collateral and restoring system health.

5. Aave Risk Management Parameters
File: AaveFeatures.ts
This section validates the newly integrated Aave-style risk models:
- Supply and Borrow Caps: Configured maximum limits for deposits and borrows. Confirmed that any transaction exceeding these caps is reverted, preventing severe liquidity risks from oversized single-asset exposure.
- Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limits: Validated the decoupling of LTV and Liquidation Threshold. Set LTV to 50% and Liquidation Threshold to 80%. Confirmed that new borrows are strictly capped at the 50% LTV mark, even if the account remains healthy.
- Health Factor: Simulated a market crash and confirmed that when a user's Health Factor drops below 1.0, third-party liquidators are authorized to repay the debt and receive collateral plus the designated liquidation bonus.

6. Emergency Market Pause Mechanism
File: AaveFeatures.ts
Tested the implemented circuit breaker pattern in the Comptroller:
- Invoked the pause functionality as the administrator.
- Verified that all core operations (mint, redeem, borrow, repay, liquidate) are successfully blocked and reverted with a explicit error.
- Confirmed that normal operations resume immediately after unpausing the market.

7. Oracle History and Frontend Dashboard Integration
File: scripts/deploy-and-feed-oracle.ts & frontend/index.html
- Upgraded the MockPriceOracle contract to store an array of historical prices alongside block timestamps.
- Wrote a deployment script to simulate and inject multi-hour price fluctuations into the local node.
- Developed a local web dashboard (frontend/index.html) using Ethers.js and Chart.js to fetch the on-chain history array and dynamically render the price trend, successfully demonstrating full-stack integration.
